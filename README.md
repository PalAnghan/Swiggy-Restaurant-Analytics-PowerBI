# 🍔 Swiggy Restaurant Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Swiggy restaurant data across 9 Indian cities, focusing on restaurant distribution, cuisine availability, pricing, customer ratings and delivery performance.

This project demonstrates how raw restaurant data can be transformed into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations and interactive visualizations.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

![Swiggy Restaurant Analytics Dashboard](dashboard-preview.jpeg)

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze restaurant distribution across Indian cities.
- Compare average restaurant ratings and delivery times.
- Identify the most widely available cuisines.
- Understand restaurant pricing and rating categories.
- Enable interactive, data-driven business exploration.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- **Restaurant Distribution:** Kolkata has the highest restaurant count in the dataset, with 1,346 restaurants, followed by Mumbai with 1,277.
- **Customer Ratings:** Chennai has the highest average rating of 3.78, followed by Bangalore at 3.76.
- **Delivery Performance:** Ahmedabad records the fastest average delivery time of 44.71 minutes, while Kolkata records the longest at 67.81 minutes.
- **Cuisine Availability:** Chinese cuisine has the highest restaurant representation, appearing across 2,588 restaurants.
- **Pricing Analysis:** Mid-range restaurants represent approximately 56.56% of the dataset, followed by budget restaurants at 31.69%.
- **Rating Analysis:** Approximately 38.18% of restaurants fall into the Below 3 rating category, highlighting an area for further investigation.
